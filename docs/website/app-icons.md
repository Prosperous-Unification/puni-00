# Build app icons

The Build app's favicons in `apps/website/fe-01/public/` are the PUNI moon alone, drawn as a
full moon with no letter. They are small
derivatives of a NASA photograph, not of the licensed theme media, so they may live in this
public repository.

- Source: NASA image `iss071e113312`, <https://images.nasa.gov/details/iss071e113312>.
- Credit: NASA.
- Rights: images.nasa.gov states that NASA material is generally not subject to copyright in
  the United States. NASA's media usage guidelines still forbid implying NASA endorsement.
- Generated by the PUNI site's favicon script on 2026-10-06 and copied byte for byte. The
  photograph is a waning gibbous; the script evens the shadowed rim so the disc reads as full,
  without painting new detail. `favicon.ico` and `favicon-32.png` are transparent;
  `apple-touch-icon.png` is opaque on #0b0915.
