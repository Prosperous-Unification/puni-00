- FALSE outright: DeepSeek V4 Pro peak ($20.72, 1.10x DEARER than Sonnet 5, +77% inside Twilight), MiniMax M3 ($21.12, 1.12x dearer, +44% inside Twilight), Kimi K3 ($22.97, dearer than Fable 5.1 and 1.22x dearer than Sonnet 5 — its owner price is 1.5x Sonnet 5's).
- FALSE against every open model: GPT-5.6 Sol at $2.78 (3.7x-8.3x cheaper than the whole open field), which is a flagship.

Two words in P3 fail: "much" (2x, not 5-10x, and only against the models Twilight would not use for implement anyway) and "flagship" (the comparison that matters is against the cheapest capable closed model, not the most expensive one).

## Model consequence

FOR THE SYNTHESIS — what changes in the cost model and the plan:

A. USE PROCESSED-TOKEN, NOT LIST-PRICE, RATIOS. The list-price blend compresses hard once cache is modelled. GLM-5.3 vs Fable 5.1: 9.3x on sticker, 2.1x per accepted feature. GLM-5.3 vs Sonnet 5: 1.86x on sticker, 1.03x per PASS and 1.81x per accepted feature (all of the latter coming from pass@1, not price). Any slide using $/MTok blends overstates the open-weight case by 3-9x.

B. THE CACHE-READ LINE IS THE WHOLE BILL AND ANTHROPIC PRICES IT BEST. At h=0.90 the share of the input bill charged as cache reads is Fable 5.1 15%, Opus 5 42%, Sonnet 5 42%, Kimi K3 42%, GLM-5.3 57%. Cache-read-to-input ratios: Fable 5.1 0.025x, Anthropic/OpenAI/Kimi 0.10x, GLM-5.3 0.186x. The open owners discount the RARE token and Anthropic discounts the COMMON one. Cache-read price parity is not a curiosity: Fable 5.1 $0.25 and Sonnet 5 $0.20 both UNDERCUT GLM-5.3's $0.26.

C. OUTPUT PRICE IS WHERE THE REAL FLAGSHIP PREMIUM LIVES, AND IT IS ONLY A QUARTER OF THE BILL. Output share of the feature cost: Fable 5.1 34%, Opus 5 26%, Sonnet 5 26%, GPT-5.6 Sol 26%, Kimi K2.6 19%, GLM-5.3 14%, MiniMax M3 16%. Fable 5.1's $50/MTok vs GLM's $4.40 is 11.4x but converts to only ~$6 per feature because the open model emits 2.19x more of it.

D. TWILIGHT'S OWN BUDGET CAP BINDS BEFORE PRICE DOES. Balanced ships 1,500,000 tokens/run. Uncached+output tokens for one p50 medium feature: Fable-5 efficiency 1.15M (fits), GPT-5.6 Sol 0.28M (5.4x headroom), Opus 5 1.98M (1.3x over), Sonnet 5 2.13M (1.4x over), DeepSeek V4 Pro 1.81M (1.2x over), GLM-5.3 2.52M (1.7x over), Kimi K2.6 2.53M (1.7x over), MiniMax M3 6.34M (4.2x over). If "tokens" includes cache reads (adapter-defined, undecided), the baseline alone is 10.15M = 6.8x over and MiniMax M3 is 56M = 37x over. RECOMMENDATION: settle the cache-read counting convention BEFORE the cap is calibrated, and re-derive the caps from M1 ledgers; a token-hungry model multiplies the binding constraint 1.6-5.5x.

E. RANK THE ADOPTION ORDER BY MEASURED SAVING PER ADAPTER-HOUR, NOT BY $/MTok. Implement-class swap, saving per accepted medium feature vs the shipped all-Anthropic balanced ($19.87): GPT-5.6 Sol $9.76 (-49%), GLM-5.3 $6.71 (-34%), Kimi K2.6 $0.70 (-3.5%), MiniMax M3 -$8.80 (worse), DeepSeek V4 Pro -$11.23 to -$15.37 (worse). Break-even against a 24-48 h adapter at $100-300/operator-hour: Sol 246-1,475 features, GLM-5.3 358-2,146 features. THE CHEAPEST NEXT PROVIDER IS THE ONE TWILIGHT ALREADY PLANS TO BUILD — Codex/ACP is named as the second probe in the adapter task; it needs no new licence, has independent same-harness scores at or above every open model, and saves 1.45x more per feature than the best open model. Build the Codex adapter first; treat open weights as a THIRD adapter justified by lock-in hedging, not by cost.

F. IF OPEN WEIGHTS ARE ADOPTED, MOVE THE VERIFIER OFF THE MODEL. The whole refutation in (3) is that Twilight bills the attempts multiplier at claude-opus-5 review+judge prices ($4.49/round). Twilight's floor ALREADY makes verification.gate, verification.browser, acceptance.evaluate and acceptance.coverage deterministic tools with zero model tokens. Every attempt an open model can be selected on by a TOOL rather than by a judge costs implement price only: DeepSeek V4 Pro off-peak at $0.83/attempt buys five tool-verified attempts for less than one Sonnet 5 attempt ($4.29). That, not the price sheet, is the design decision that would make P3 true — and it is exactly what R5 already demands (a check that provably fails, i.e. a deterministic oracle).

G. DO NOT LET A CHEAP MODEL ESCALATE INTO A FLAGSHIP. balanced escalates a failed implement to claude-opus-5 with the failed attempt still charged. Pairing a high-attempts open model with an opus-5 escalation target is the worst of both: you pay the cheap failures AND the expensive success. If an open implement class is introduced, its escalation target should be sonnet-5 or a second open model, and rework.maxRounds must be raised above the model's attempts multiplier or the run will pause on exhaustion (DeepSeek V4 Pro at a~5 cannot be accepted under balanced's cap of ~4 implement attempts at all).

H. THE ECONOMICS ARE ROUNDING ERROR AGAINST REVENUE. Model cost per accepted medium feature is $2.78-$24.94 against a $1,500-$8,000 price band: 0.03%-1.7% of revenue. The Opus-5-to-GLM-5.3 delta is 0.2%-1.0% of revenue, or 3-9 operator-minutes at $100-300/hour. Cost per accepted outcome must include unaccepted runs (design.md:744-748); a model that raises the pause rate costs operator time, and one 15-minute pause ($25-75) erases 4-11 features of saving. OPTIMISE FOR ACCEPTANCE RATE AND FEWER HUMAN PAUSES, NOT FOR $/MTok.

I. CARRY THE UNKNOWNS AS UNKNOWNS. (i) No self-hosting sheet exists — owned-GPU open-weight economics are PENDING and none of the above covers them. (ii) m for GLM-5.3, Kimi K3 and Kimi K2.6 are ASSUMED (2.19 borrowed from GLM-5.2, 2.20 invented for the Kimis); no same-scaffold measurement exists for any current open flagship. (iii) The 0.24x multiplier for GPT-5.6 Sol is the single most load-bearing number in the table and comes from one source at "medium" effort; at m=1.0 Sol becomes $16.20 and GLM-5.3 wins by 1.6x. (iv) GPT-5.6 Sol's $4/$20 is promotional through at least 2026-11-21. (v) The mandated 1.25x cache-write premium is an Anthropic/OpenAI convention; open owners generally bill a cache MISS at plain input price, so their figures above are overstated by 20% of the uncached-input term (~2-5% of total) — it does not change any ranking.

## Decisive signal

ONE MEASUREMENT, AVAILABLE FROM TWILIGHT'S OWN M1 LEDGER, SETTLES BOTH HALVES.

Primary (internal, cheapest, decides the price half): run Task 8's two-profile fixture twice — once shipped (implement=claude-sonnet-5) and once with implement=GLM-5.3 through the new adapter — and read three ledger quantities that execution.yaml already requires (input/output/cacheRead/cacheWrite tokens, activity attempts, human minutes):
(a) MEASURED CACHE-HIT RATE h ON THE OPEN PROVIDER. If h >= 93.3%, GLM-5.3 costs MORE per pass than Sonnet 5 and P3's price half is dead in this harness. The crossover is exact: 46.25(1-h)+3.7h+2.775 = 38.325(1-h)+5.694h+1.445 => h = 0.933. Claude-Code-class harnesses measure 91-97%, so the null hypothesis is that P3 fails.
(b) MEASURED TOKEN MULTIPLIER m FOR GLM-5.3 ON A REAL FEATURE. P3 survives if m <= 2.2; it becomes strong if m <= 1.5; it dies if m >= 3.
(c) MEASURED ATTEMPTS-TO-ACCEPT a. P3 dies at a >= 2 under any profile whose review+judge is a flagship model, because one opus-5 review round ($4.49) costs more than a whole GLM-5.3 implement attempt ($4.16).
