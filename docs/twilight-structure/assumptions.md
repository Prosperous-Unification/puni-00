## Stress-test answers

- **Approval arrives after the plan changed:** reject it as stale, preserve the
  attempted decision, and show the new diff. An assumption cannot refresh it.
- **Worker dies after a tool changed an external system:** reconcile the recorded
  effect identity before retry; unknown outcome pauses that activity. A restart
  does not authorize a second effect.
- **Safety critic and implementation critic disagree:** record both findings,
  judge against a pinned rubric, retain unresolved mandatory findings, and stop
  on the review limit. Majority voting cannot override a denied capability.
- **The user wants fewer interruptions:** change presentation and authorized
  checkpoint policy; collect decisions at a coherent boundary. Silence or elapsed
  time never supplies a required approval.
- **Research says a spec is wrong:** open a contradiction and propose a delta.
  The wiki may record evidence immediately; it cannot silently edit the contract.
- **Budget is consumed while waiting for a reviewer:** waiting, reserved capacity,
  actual consumption, and projected spend remain separate. A wait must have a
  visible reason, deadline, and cancellation path.

## Wayfinding question register

Recorded 2026-09-08 after Dany asked the agent to keep moving through assumptions,
score the unanswered questions, and bring back only the most important ones. A
score estimates the cost of a wrong assumption and how soon it blocks useful work:

- **90–100:** ask Dany before the affected phase or irreversible choice starts.
- **70–89:** proceed with the recommendation, but validate it before implementing
  the named boundary.
- **40–69:** use the default until evidence or use makes it inconvenient.
- **1–39:** presentation or operational tuning; change without replanning the route.

An importance score is triage, not confidence. Every entry remains an assumption,
cannot authorize spending or release, and is superseded by a contrary user choice.
