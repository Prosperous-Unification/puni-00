# Personal assistant

The person's conversational contact across requests of any kind, including work
delegated to agents and software delivery through Twilight Structure.

## Language

**Secretary**:
The person's default assistant across requests and continuing point of contact
for work delegated to other agents.
_Avoid_: Twilight coordinator, software-delivery role

**Worker**:
A recurring agent identity across assignments, with conversations the person can
join directly. Its name is freely changeable by the person without changing its
identity or the ownership of its history and assignments.
_Avoid_: Job title, assignment, session, Twilight execution worker

**Assignment**:
A piece of work entrusted to a worker, distinct from the worker's recurring
identity and the conversations used to carry it out.
_Avoid_: Worker, session
