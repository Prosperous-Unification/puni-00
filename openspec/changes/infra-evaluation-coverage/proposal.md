## Why

The infrastructure coverage research records complete committed inventory but
cannot prove which registered rules actually judged each subject. Its disposable
comparisons are not production enforcement. A reusable diagnostic must refuse
stale authority and preserve unsupported semantics as unevaluated.

## What Changes

Add `inspect-evaluation-coverage <repository> <full-sha>
<external-rule-policy> <external-coverage-review>`. Its separate version 1 report
binds complete committed inventory, explicit nonempty coverage subjects, all registered
rules, actual runtime artifacts, exact policy bytes and an explicit adopted set.
Each subject/rule pair records applicability separately from evaluation. Only
engine observations within concrete rule footprints establish evaluation;
coverage exclusions require an attributed reason and reference.

## Non-Goals

No activation, admission, native infrastructure execution, live health judgment,
deployment, infrastructure semantic evaluator, support YAML dependency repair or
WBS120 completion. Existing check verdicts retain their schema. Reports always
state `allowed: false` and `certifies: false`.

## Constraints

Reuse candidate selection, external artifact reads, policy validation, registry
and check execution. Require a full committed identity and every registered mode.
Never infer semantic evaluation from classification or absence of findings.
Keep focused tests serial; full local Nx, Burokrat and browser suites are excluded
by the task's memory constraint. WBS120 remains `blocked_by_proxy`.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `evaluation-coverage`: committed inventory and exact rule/subject coverage joins.

### Modified Capabilities

None.

## Domain Terms

Evaluation coverage, Coverage subject, Coverage exclusion; recorded in
[Twilight Structure](../../../docs/twilight-structure/CONTEXT.md).

## Decisions Recorded

None. The diagnostic is additive and reversible.

## Impact

Twilight Burokrat CLI, internal shared check execution and focused fixtures.
No deployment, database or authority writer changes.
