## Decisions recorded at review (2026-10-12)

- **A42 (Fable, binding)**: attempt numbers are never reused; `step_node_attempt_issued` is the
  high-water mark (design D1 lists how each path keeps it). The hand-down **copies** marks and
  keeps the parent's, the simplest rule under which the create's undo and redo cannot reissue a
  number; restores of deleted branches carry the marks in `issuedAttemptNumbers`.
- **`node_done` inside the transaction**: the service's check stays as the fast path; the
  store's read inside `BEGIN IMMEDIATE` is the refusal a second process cannot slip past in a
  blue/green overlap (design D2).
