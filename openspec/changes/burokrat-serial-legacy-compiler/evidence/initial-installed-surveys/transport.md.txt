# Packet integrity and replay

Files ending in `.txt` preserve original bytes, including JSON and Python source.
[source-map.json.txt](source-map.json.txt) maps copied files to their original
absolute paths and SHA-256 identities. No display whitespace was normalized.
[raw-streams.json.txt](raw-streams.json.txt) additionally preserves all 64 stdout
and stderr streams as lossless UTF-8 strings with byte counts and hashes.
Negative diagnostics are concatenated for reading; the raw-stream entries retain
exact individual streams, including empty stdout.

The nineteen copied negative datasets are intentionally omitted. The original
five reports, receipts and snapshots, unchanged policy, exact
[negative reproducer](prove-comparator.py.txt), commands and per-fault changed-input
hashes reconstruct each injected fault. The reproducer retains its original
absolute paths. Replay requires restoring the original layout in a disposable
location or explicitly adapting those paths, plus access to the exact installed
successor and repository objects. Do not execute it over the existing proof
folder: it refuses to replace that folder. These dependencies are not bundled.

`compare-surveys-usage.txt` is the original preparation document; its statement
that proofs were pending describes that earlier checkpoint. The completed
comparison and negative receipts in this packet supersede that status only.
The restored pristine command/output are under `negatives/`.

`manifest.json.txt` hashes every other packet file. `integrity-check.json.txt`
records source-byte equality, raw-stream round trips, nineteen named failures,
unchanged original hashes and refreshed compatibility parity. No new compression,
repository source change or evidence reclassification was performed.
