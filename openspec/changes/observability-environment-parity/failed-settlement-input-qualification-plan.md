## Can the existing 160MiB contract already prove the whole launch?

No. The available contracts supply useful components but not the sum. They contain bounded owned captures and native control metadata/fixtures; the settlement publisher has separate K. They contain no observed effective audit/journal/fsnotify consumer cap that closes the remaining gross causal term. This is absence of supplied proof, not evidence of unbounded actual activity or evidence that such effective limits do not exist.

For scale only, the dated b9 admission records `outside + future = 146960384`, leaving `20811776` bytes under `167772160`. That is about 19.85MiB, not fresh headroom. The earlier raw-repair preflight records `107315200 + 35717120 = 143032320`; it is also historical and describes a different future envelope. Neither can be transplanted into settlement admission. Current retained reviews and new qualification material reduce available headroom.

Require an explicit nonoverlapping calculation:

`current owned outside allocation + committed future captures/metadata + qualified future causal input/runtime/consumer allocation <= 167772160`.

Existing OS/library occupancy remains in host free-space occupancy; do not charge their whole installed size again. Charge operation-caused additional allocation wherever its consumers persist it. Distinguish capacity headroom from cumulative gross debt. An existing hard per-store capacity could bound simultaneous occupancy if its semantics include all active/archive/rotation files and queues; it cannot silently erase cumulative allocations or unreturned failed allocations from this contract. A finite event-volume bound plus the store's reviewed allocation behavior may suffice without changing any policy. No such number is supplied here.
