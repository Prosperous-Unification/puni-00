## Why

The installed whole-tree survey refuses five existing website binary assets because they lack exact declarations. This prevents inventory classification from evaluating the otherwise unchanged candidate.

## What Changes

Declare the three website icons and two redistributable fonts with exact paths, formats, consumers and existing provenance sources. Record exact font sizes and hashes beside their license notices. Prove the production CLI and installed package accept these files and still refuse omissions, undeclared neighbors and text contradictions.

## Non-Goals

No classifier changes, broad binary allowlists, runtime digest enforcement, asset changes, policy mode changes, activation, deployment or unrelated survey repairs.

## Constraints

Preserve every policy field outside the five added declarations. Keep the original external diagnostic policy immutable and derive its successor by the same five additions. Compare the same committed candidate with the same installed tool. Raw survey transcripts remain local; commit sanitized evidence only. The approved architecture packet resolves the design scope; no new domain terms or ADR are needed.
